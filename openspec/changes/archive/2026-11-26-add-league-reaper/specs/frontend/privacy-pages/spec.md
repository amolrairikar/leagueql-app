## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Disclose unused-league retention
The general privacy page SHALL state that league data is retained until the league's owner deletes it, the user asks for its removal, or nobody has opened the league for one year, and that stored platform credentials may be removed along with an unused league.

#### Scenario: Retention disclosure
- **WHEN** the general privacy page renders
- **THEN** its retention statement includes automatic removal after one year without the league being opened
