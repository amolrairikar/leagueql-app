## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: FAQ explains unused-league removal
The landing-page FAQ SHALL include a question about whether a league is ever removed, whose answer states that a league nobody has opened for one year (counted from onboarding if never opened) is permanently removed, that automatic refreshes do not count, and that it can be connected again, with a link to `/docs`.

#### Scenario: Removal question expanded
- **WHEN** a visitor expands "Will my league ever be removed?"
- **THEN** the answer states the one-year rule, that automatic refreshes do not count, that the league can be connected again, and links to the docs
